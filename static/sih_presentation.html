1. Proposed Solution & Value Proposition
Democratizing Government Subsidies for Grassroots Founders
⚠ The Grassroots Challenge
  • Massive Information Asymmetry: 150+ Central & State schemes exist, yet 85% of micro-entrepreneurs never apply due to bureaucratic jargon.
  • Linguistic & Literacy Divide: Official portals publish guidelines in formal English/Hindi, excluding regional vernacular and low-literacy founders.
  • Middlemen Exploitation: Lack of transparent document checklists and official links forces applicants to pay 10-20% cuts to touts.
  • Complex Eligibility Matrix: Age caps, greenfield limits, category reservations, and district boundaries confuse first-time applicants.
✔ The Udhyami Yojna Innovation
  • Multilingual Voice Autofill: Speaks naturally in 12 Indian languages; browser speech recognition populates forms in seconds.
  • AI Extraction with Zero-Crash Guarantee: Extracts 11 structured parameters; falls back gracefully to bilingual regex if offline.
  • Deterministic Python Scoring Engine: Source-of-truth rule engine eliminates AI hallucinations regarding money and criteria.
  • Plain-Language Guidance: Translates dense official notifications into empathetic, actionable next steps.
  • 28 States & UTs Regional Hub: Interactive directory linking district craft clusters and DIC single-window clearance links.
