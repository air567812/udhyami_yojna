🏛️ About Udhyami Yojna | राष्ट्रीय उद्यम एवं योजना पोर्टल

Digital Public Infrastructure for Enterprise Inclusion

Udhyami Yojna is an innovative, citizen-centric Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) platform aligned with the National Portal of India (india.gov.in) and the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India.

Core Architectural Pillars

  • Vernacular Voice Ingestion: Multilingual speech recognition across 12 official Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, English) breaking literacy barriers.
  • Deterministic Gazette Rule Engine: 100-point transparent algorithmic scoring based strictly on official Central and State Government Gazette criteria, ensuring complete equity and zero bias.
  • End-to-End Linkage: Seamless integration with Udyam Registration, JanSamarth National Credit Portal, and District Industries Centres (DICs).
  • Affirmative Financial Inclusion: Specialized subsidies and grant calculation for Women, Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Divyangjan, and traditional rural artisans.

Institutional Compliance

Maintained strictly following the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0), ISO 27001 cybersecurity frameworks, and WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance.

📜 Website Policies | पोर्टल नीतियां (GIGW 3.0)

Hyperlinking Policy

Prior permission is not required to link directly to the information hosted on this portal. However, pages must load into a full newly opened browser window and not within framing structures. All outbound external links are provided for citizen convenience with explicit notification.

Copyright Policy

Material featured on this portal may be reproduced free of charge in any format or media without special permission, provided the material is reproduced accurately and not used in a derogatory or misleading manner. The source must be prominently acknowledged.

Privacy Policy

This portal does not capture any personal identifying information (PII) without voluntary citizen consent. Spoken voice transcripts and demographic variables entered for scheme discovery are evaluated ephemerally in-session and are never sold or shared with commercial entities.

Content Contribution & Validation Policy

All scheme rules, subsidy percentages, and nodal bank criteria are synchronized with official gazetted notifications published by the Ministry of MSME, Ministry of Finance, and state industry departments.

❓ Help & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) | प्रश्नोत्तरी

1. How does Udhyami Yojna calculate eligible schemes?

The portal uses an objective, gazette-verified rule engine. Your age, investment requirements, enterprise sector, geographic location (Urban/Rural), and social category are compared against official subsidy parameters to calculate a match score and actionable roadmap.

2. Do I need collateral security to apply for loans?

Under major initiatives like PMEGP, Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana, and CGTMSE, loans up to ₹5 Crore are collateral-free, backed by government credit guarantee trusts.

3. Who qualifies for the higher 35% subsidy grant?

Under PMEGP and respective state industrial schemes, enterprises set up in Rural areas by Women, SC, ST, OBC, Divyangjan, or Ex-Servicemen qualify for up to 35% margin money capital grants.

4. Where do I apply after discovering my schemes?

Each scheme card provides an official direct link to the statutory application portal (such as JanSamarth.in, KVIC PMEGP Portal, or Udyam Registration).
